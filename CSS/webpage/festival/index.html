THE FESTIVAL

The Festival
of Beginnings

Ganesh Chaturthi is a celebration of wisdom, prosperity and new beginnings.

Families and communities welcome Lord Ganesha with devotion, prayers, music, flowers and sweet offerings. For a few beautiful days, homes and public spaces become places of togetherness, faith and celebration.
